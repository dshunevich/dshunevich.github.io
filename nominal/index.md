# Nominal: CBR Exchange Rates — Support

Nominal shows the official exchange rates of the Bank of Russia: today's rates and, once the Bank of Russia sets them,
tomorrow's; a converter between any two currencies (cross rates via the ruble), rate history charts up to 5 years, rates
for any past date and precious-metal prices, plus a Home Screen widget with your favourite currencies. The app is in
Russian. It is free and supported by ads from Yandex Advertising Network; there are no purchases.

## Contact
Questions, bugs or ideas: **dmitriyshunevich@gmail.com** — we answer within 2 business days.
In the app: Rates → (i) → "Написать разработчику".

## Report an ad
If you saw an inappropriate ad, write to dmitriyshunevich@gmail.com with a short description (a screenshot helps) —
in the app: Rates → (i) → "Написать разработчику".

## FAQ
**Where do the rates come from?** From the public files on www.cbr.ru. The app is not affiliated with the Bank of Russia
and is not investment advice; exchange rates at banks differ from the official rate.

**When do tomorrow's rates appear?** The Bank of Russia usually publishes them in the second half of a working day; then
a "Сегодня / Завтра" switch appears on the Rates screen.

**Does it work offline?** Yes: the last downloaded rates are saved on your device, and the app shows when they were updated.

**How do I set up the widget?** Add the "Номинал" widget to the Home Screen: the small one shows your first two favourite
currencies, the medium one shows four. Change the order with "Править" in the list. There are no ads in the widget.

# Номинал: курсы валют ЦБ — поддержка

«Номинал» показывает официальные курсы валют Банка России: на сегодня и, как только ЦБ их установит, на завтра;
конвертер любых валют (кросс-курсы через рубль), графики курса до 5 лет, курсы на любую дату, учётные цены драгметаллов
и виджет с избранными валютами. Приложение бесплатное, работает за счёт рекламы Рекламной сети Яндекса; покупок нет.
Данные: Банк России. Не является инвестиционной рекомендацией.
