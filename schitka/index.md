# Schitka: QR & Barcode Scanner — Support

Schitka is a QR and barcode scanner and generator that understands Russian codes: payment QR codes on utility bills
(GOST R 56042) with checksum verification of the account and tax ID, fiscal receipt codes, "Chestny ZNAK" marking codes,
EAN barcodes, Wi‑Fi, contacts and more. Links are checked before opening and look-alike domains are flagged. Codes are
recognised on your device. The app is in Russian. It is free and supported by ads from Yandex Advertising Network; there
are no purchases.

## Contact
Questions, bugs or ideas: **dmitriyshunevich@gmail.com** — we answer within 2 business days.
In the app: Scanner → (i) → "Написать разработчику".

## Report an ad
If you saw an inappropriate ad, write to dmitriyshunevich@gmail.com with a short description (a screenshot helps) —
in the app: Scanner → (i) → "Написать разработчику".

## FAQ
**Can I pay a bill in Schitka?** No. Schitka only shows and checks the payment details in the code; pay in your bank's app.
It is not affiliated with any bank.

**What permissions does it need?** Only the camera. Photos are picked with the system photo picker; add-only access to
Photos is asked only if you save a generated code to Photos.

**Where is my history stored?** Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

**Does a barcode show the product name?** No: there is no product database. Schitka shows the number, checks the check
digit and offers a web search.

# Считка: сканер QR и штрихкодов — поддержка

«Считка» — сканер и генератор QR-кодов и штрихкодов, который понимает российские коды: платёжные QR на квитанциях
(ГОСТ Р 56042) с проверкой счёта и ИНН по контрольным суммам, чеки, «Честный ЗНАК», штрихкоды EAN, Wi‑Fi, контакты и
другое. Ссылки проверяются до перехода, сайты-двойники отмечаются. Распознавание — на телефоне. Приложение бесплатное,
работает за счёт рекламы Рекламной сети Яндекса; покупок нет. Считка не проводит платежи и не связана с банками.
