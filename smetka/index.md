# Smetka: Loan Calculator — Support

Smetka is a loan, mortgage and deposit calculator for Russia: a payment schedule to the kopeck with real payment dates
(annuity or differentiated, interest by actual days or rate / 12), early repayment (reduce the term or the payment),
deposits with capitalisation, top-ups and withdrawals and the personal income tax on deposit interest, a side-by-side
comparison of 2–3 offers, saved calculations and PDF/CSV export. Everything is calculated on your device. The app is in
Russian. It is free and supported by ads from Yandex Advertising Network; there are no purchases.

## Contact
Questions, bugs or ideas: **dmitriyshunevich@gmail.com** — we answer within 2 business days.
In the app: Loan → (i) → "Написать разработчику".

## Report an ad
If you saw an inappropriate ad, write to dmitriyshunevich@gmail.com with a short description (a screenshot helps) —
in the app: Loan → (i) → "Написать разработчику".

## FAQ
**Why does my bank's payment differ by a few rubles?** Banks round and count days differently. Smetka shows the method it
used (actual days 365/366 or rate / 12) on the screen, in the schedule and in the PDF; see (i) → "Как считается". The
calculation is approximate and is not an offer or financial advice.

**Does the app offer loans?** No. It is not affiliated with any bank and contains no affiliate links.

**Where are my saved calculations?** Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

# Сметка: кредитный калькулятор — поддержка

«Сметка» — кредитный и ипотечный калькулятор и калькулятор вкладов: точный график платежей с датами, досрочное
погашение, вклад с капитализацией и НДФЛ, сравнение 2–3 предложений, сохранённые расчёты и экспорт в PDF/CSV. Всё
считается на телефоне. Приложение бесплатное, работает за счёт рекламы Рекламной сети Яндекса; покупок нет.
Расчёт приблизительный, не является офертой или финансовой рекомендацией.
