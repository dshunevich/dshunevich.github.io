# Pyatidnevka: Russian Production Calendar — Support

Pyatidnevka is the official Russian production calendar for the five-day working week: working days, weekends, public
holidays, moved days off and shortened pre-holiday days for 2024–2027, standard working hours for 40-, 36- and 24-hour
weeks, a calculator of working days and hours between two dates, a vacation planner and Home Screen widgets. The app is
in Russian and works offline. It is free and supported by ads from Yandex Advertising Network; there are no purchases.

## FAQ
**Where does the data come from?** From the Labour Code of the Russian Federation (Art. 112) and the Government decrees on
moving days off; the number and date of each decree are shown in the app (Today → (i) → "О данных"). This is a reference
app, not legal advice, and it is not affiliated with any government body.

**How does a new year get added?** About once a week the app checks one public calendar file on this site and adds a new
year when a decree is published. The file contains only calendar data.

**Are regional holidays included?** No. Holidays of individual republics (for example, Tatarstan and Bashkortostan) are
set every year by separate acts and are not included.

**Are there ads in the widgets?** No.

# Пятидневка: производственный календарь — поддержка

«Пятидневка» — производственный календарь России для пятидневной рабочей недели: рабочие, выходные и праздничные дни,
переносы и сокращённые дни 2024–2027, нормы часов при 40-, 36- и 24-часовой неделе, расчёт рабочих дней и часов между
датами, планировщик отпуска и виджеты. Работает без интернета. Приложение бесплатное, работает за счёт рекламы Рекламной
сети Яндекса; покупок нет. Справочное приложение, не юридическая консультация.
